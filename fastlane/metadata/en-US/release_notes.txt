This update adds a cyclometer, ride sharing, and smarter e-bike filtering.

- Cyclometer mode: switch to a big-number speedometer while riding; the screen stays awake, with an optional vibrate alert when you pass a speed you set
- Auto-pause at stops: waiting at a red light no longer counts toward your ride time
- Ride share card: turn a ride into a clean route-silhouette card and share it (no map streets — your exact location isn't exposed)
- Speed curve & elevation: the detail page now shows your speed over time and total climb
- Merge tracks: long-press to merge a ride that got split into two
- Smarter e-bike filtering: unusually fast, steady rides get a "suspected e-bike" badge you can exclude in one swipe; Settings also lets you choose which activity types to auto-record

Everything runs on device and is only a hint — nothing is deleted automatically. You always decide.
