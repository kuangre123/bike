Fixes an issue where route warnings and total climb did not appear in 1.4. After planning a route you will now see steep climbs, unpaved sections, busy roads and more.

Voice navigation is also improved: turn prompts now come from the routing engine and only fire at real junctions, so winding roads no longer trigger constant announcements. Adds "keep left / keep right" and roundabout exit prompts. The voice now follows your system language.
