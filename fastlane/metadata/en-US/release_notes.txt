This update brings in workouts from other watches and apps, plans routes by bike type, and can read out your progress while you ride.

- Import workouts from other devices: rides that Garmin Connect, Huawei Health, Zepp, Wahoo, Keep, Strava and others write to Apple Health can be imported by source in Settings — no separate logins. Read-only: deleting a record here never touches their data
- Live Apple Watch heart rate: your watch's reading shows on the phone right away, instead of waiting for a background sync
- Route planning by bike type: road bike, mountain bike and gravel each get their own routing
- Warnings before you set off: steep climbs, unpaved surfaces, busy roads without a bike lane, and footpaths you'd have to push through
- Spoken updates while riding: every 10 minutes and every 5 km it reads out duration, distance and average speed. Music is ducked, not cut off. Can be turned off in Settings
- Share cards now include the map: your route is drawn on real streets
- More accurate cyclometer timing at stops
- When a ride has no route, the app now explains why — background route recording needs "Always" location access

About the cyclometer: manual cyclometer mode is now a Pro feature, with one full free ride for everyone. Passive auto-recording, stats and sharing remain free.
