This update keeps e-bike trips out of your cycling stats.

- Suspected e-bike detection: rides that look unusually fast and steady get a subtle "suspected e-bike" badge on the timeline
- One-swipe exclusion: swipe a ride (or tap Exclude on its detail page) to mark it as an e-bike trip — it's removed from your home card, weekly stats, and watch summary, and the workout is deleted from Apple Health
- Fully reversible: excluded rides stay in a collapsed section at the bottom of the list and can be restored anytime, with automatic write-back to Apple Health if enabled
- New setting: "Auto-flag suspected e-bikes" (on by default), easy to turn off

Detection runs entirely on device and is only a hint — nothing is ever deleted automatically. You always make the call.

Thanks for riding with HappyRide. If a ride gets flagged wrongly, let us know — your feedback helps us tune the thresholds.
